How to Use This Generator

  1. Adjust the length: Drag the slider to set your desired password length. Longer passwords are more secure.
  2. Select character types: Check the boxes to include uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols. More variety means stronger passwords.
  3. Generate: Click the "Generate Password" button to create a new random password.
  4. Copy: Click "Copy to Clipboard" to easily paste your password where needed.
  5. Save securely: Store your password in a password manager or write it down and keep it in a secure location.

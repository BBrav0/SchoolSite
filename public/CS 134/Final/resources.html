🔐 Password Managers

Password managers are essential tools for maintaining unique, strong passwords across all your accounts. Here are the most trusted options:

Bitwarden

Best for: Everyone, especially budget-conscious users

Price: Free / $10/year premium

Features: Open source, unlimited passwords, cross-platform sync, password generator, secure sharing

Visit Bitwarden

1Password

Best for: Families and teams

Price: $36/year individual, $60/year family

Features: Excellent UI, travel mode, watchtower alerts, secure document storage

Visit 1Password

LastPass

Best for: Free tier users

Price: Free / $36/year premium

Features: Easy to use, auto-fill, password health check, emergency access

Visit LastPass

Dashlane

Best for: Premium features

Price: $60/year

Features: VPN included, dark web monitoring, password changer, identity theft protection

Visit Dashlane

KeePass

Best for: Tech-savvy, privacy-focused users

Price: Free (open source)

Features: Completely offline, highly customizable, plugins available, full control

Visit KeePass

NordPass

Best for: NordVPN users

Price: Free / $18/year premium

Features: Modern interface, XChaCha20 encryption, password health, data breach scanner

Visit NordPass
